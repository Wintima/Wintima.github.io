School Refurbishment — Yizidug Basic School

Yizidug, Upper East Region, Ghana

Yizidug Basic School currently faces serious infrastructural challenges. Broken classroom doors, damaged windows, and leaking roofs are adversely affecting teaching and learning. At present, pupils sit on the floor during lessons — a condition that undermines their ability to focus and thrive.

Wintima Foundation is raising funds to fully refurbish the school — repairing doors, windows, and roofs across six classrooms, painting the classrooms, and supplying 50 dual desks so every pupil has a proper place to learn.

Estimated Cost Breakdown

Item Description Cost (GHS)
Classroom Doors Repair and replace broken doors for 6 classrooms 12,000
Windows Replacement and fixing of damaged frames 10,000
Roofing Repair of leaking roofs for classrooms 10,000
Labour & Misc. Skilled labour, transportation, painting of 6 classrooms 18,000
Total 50,000
